05 · Guardrails & Stage 3 Roadmap 3 Distinct Directions

Embrace (Skincare Poise)

  • Editorial negative space & calm pacing
  • Subtle phase geometry & light/shadow
  • Reinforced custom serif letterforms
  • Matte ivory, frosted glass, muted minerals
  • 1-color foil, emboss & 16 px durability

Strictly Avoid (Clichés)

  • Literal crescent moons & 4-point sparkles
  • Purple/indigo galaxy or aurora gradients
  • Generic botanical leaves or water drops
  • Heavy gold borders & mystical tarot vibes
  • Unmodified stock fonts or fragile hairlines
Concept A · Wordmark Luminous Wordmark

Custom serif SELUNÉA with sculpted S spine, light-sliver É accent & minimal phase dot-arc mark.

Concept B · Symbol Lunar Emblem

Abstract negative-space phase vessel built from pure circular arcs — zero clip-art, crisp at 16 px.

Concept C · Monogram Celestial Monogram

Organic-geometric custom S monogram with quiet orbital poise for caps, jar seals & favicons.
