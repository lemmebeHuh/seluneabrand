### Explicit Stage 1 Assumptions (Open for Client Confirmation)
1. **Accent Diacritic (`É`):** The acute accent over `E` is an essential part of the official brand name and a primary visual differentiator in the wordmark, while plain `selunea` is used for URLs/handles.
2. **Primary Packaging Architecture:** Clean, quiet, front-of-pack editorial hierarchy (logo + product name + category + net content placeholder) without invented active percentages, SPF claims, or unverified certifications.
3. **Brand Tone Balance:** Approximately **70% calm editorial minimalism / 30% warm feminine softness** — quiet confidence rather than cold clinical lab minimalism or overly ornate luxury.

## 2. Creative Direction: LUNAR ELEGANCE

### Concept Philosophy
Literal moons turn a skincare brand into an astrology or candle shop. Instead, **Lunar Elegance** translates the moon's physical and visual phenomena into disciplined skincare design principles:

1. **Rhythm of Phases (Progression & Cycle):** Subtle transitions from crescent to gibbous to full expressed through geometric curvature, negative-space tension, and rhythmic spacing — mirroring both lunar cycles and daily/monthly skincare routines.
2. **Reflected Light (Luminosity without Glare):** The moon does not shout; it reflects soft directional light. Visually, this translates to warm ivory negative space, pearl-like tonal restraint, and high-contrast typographic poise rather than shiny gradients or sparkles.
3. **Orbital Poise (Precision Meets Organic Flow):** A balance between mathematical circular arcs and soft feminine curves — reflecting skincare that unites thoughtful formulation with gentle, tactile care.
4. **Editorial Shelf Quietness:** In an affordable-premium skincare aisle crowded with loud neon actives, chaotic serif-sans mashups, and literal botanical drawings, disciplined restraint and tactile neutral harmony create instant shelf authority.

### Creative Pillars (Do)
- **Modern Feminine Minimalism:** Generous breathing room (clear space), unhurried layouts, poised proportions.
- **Editorial Beauty Aesthetics:** Magazine-grade typographic hierarchy pairing sculptural high-contrast serif display with clean humanist/geometric sans for functional skincare copy.
- **Subtle Celestial Geometry:** Circular intersections, negative-space phase cuts, calibrated orbital curves.
- **Organic Geometric Forms:** Softened precision — circular arcs, smooth inflection points, no harsh mechanical spikes.
- **Strong Packaging Recognition:** Marks engineered from day one to anchor tall narrow bottles, cylindrical tubes, round jar lids, and folding cartons equally well.

### Explicit Off-Limits List (Avoid)
- Generic crescent moon clip-art icons or moon-plus-star duos.
- Sparkles, four-point twinkles, constellation dots, or mystical tarot motifs.
- Purple/indigo "galaxy" gradients or holographic sheen.
- Literal botanical leaves, flowers, water droplets, or face line-art.
- Excessive metallic gold framing, filigree crests, or faux-luxury borders.
- Unmodified stock fonts passed off as a final wordmark.
- Fragile hairlines or micro-details that vanish on a 12 mm bottle cap or 16 px favicon.

### Critical Contrast Finding (Quality Gate)
- **Midnight Ash (`#34332F`)** achieves **AAA contrast (9.07:1 – 12.65:1)** on Moon Ivory, Pure White, and all three supporting pastel tints, and **AA+ (6.29:1 – 6.59:1)** against Champagne Beige and Lunar Silver.
- **Champagne Beige (`#C8B9A6`)** and **Lunar Silver (`#B8B7B1`)** drop below 2.0:1 against Moon Ivory and White. **Rule:** Never set body copy, product specs, or standalone primary logos in Champagne Beige or Lunar Silver on light backgrounds unless physically realized as high-contrast reflective foil stamping or paired with Midnight Ash.

## 4. Typography Direction (Initial Evaluation)

All five candidate families in `selenua.md` are available under the **SIL Open Font License (OFL)** via Google Fonts, making them safe for commercial brand and packaging systems (with final logo wordmarks redrawn/customized as pure vector `<path>` outlines).

### Primary Serif Candidates (Logo Inspiration & Editorial Display)
1. **Cormorant Garamond** — *High-contrast classical/editorial serif with graceful calligraphic curves.*
   - **Pros:** Effortlessly feminine, poetic, strong editorial beauty presence, refined uppercase proportions (`S`, `E`, `N`, `A`).
   - **Risks:** Very thin hairlines in light weights can break on small packaging prints or 16 px screens; requires SemiBold/Medium optical weight or custom stroke reinforcement for logo use.
2. **Bodoni Moda** — *Modern Didone with architectural vertical stress and crisp optical-size axes.*
   - **Pros:** High fashion authority, sharp geometric clarity, supports optical sizes (better small-size durability than classic Bodoni).
   - **Risks:** Can lean slightly colder/more high-fashion or fragrance-like if not softened by warm spacing and organic curves.
3. **Instrument Serif** — *Condensed editorial serif with crisp, contemporary personality.*
   - **Pros:** Distinctly modern and memorable in headlines; strong vertical presence on tall skincare bottles.
   - **Risks:** Narrow width can feel tight or overly trendy as a primary skincare wordmark; narrower character set/weight range (single weight + italic).

**Stage 1 Typographic Hypothesis:** Use **custom-drawn editorial serif geometry** (blending Cormorant Garamond's feminine curvature with Bodoni Moda's architectural poise and reinforced hairlines for packaging durability) for the brand wordmark, paired with **Cormorant Garamond** (or **Bodoni Moda**) for Display/Editorial Headlines and **DM Sans** or **Manrope** for UI, body copy, and functional skincare product information. *(DM Sans offers cleaner geometric neutrality for INCI/product labels; Manrope adds slight humanist warmth — to be head-to-head tested in Stage 2/6.)*

## 5. Strategic Direction for the Three Logo Concepts (Preview for Stage 3)

Per `selenua.md` §3 and `logo-design` Phase 2–3, Stage 3 will build **three genuinely distinct structural ideas** (designed and evaluated in pure black-and-white first):

1. **Concept A — THE LUMINOUS WORDMARK (Typography-Led):**
   - Custom high-contrast editorial serif `SELUNÉA` where the `S` spine carries a calibrated lunar-phase inflection and the acute accent on `É` is sculpted as a refined crescent-sliver / light-angle mark, accompanied by a minimal supporting phase dot/arc mark.
2. **Concept B — THE LUNAR EMBLEM (Symbol-Led Abstract Geometry):**
   - Pure negative-space geometric emblem expressing moon phases through two-to-three intersecting circles/arcs with real `evenodd` cutouts (no literal crescent clip-art), engineered to read crisply from a 16 px favicon to a frosted glass bottle front.
3. **Concept C — THE CELESTIAL MONOGRAM (Letterform / Seal-Led):**
   - Custom sculptural `S` monogram uniting organic feminine flow with structured orbital geometry — specifically engineered to shine as an embossed dropper cap seal, jar lid stamp, wax/foil carton seal, and crisp social avatar.

> **Stage 1 Boundary:** No logo concepts are drawn or finalized in Stage 1. This creative direction serves as the strategic contract to be reviewed before moving into Stage 2 (Visual Direction & Moodboard) and Stage 3 (Concept Construction).
