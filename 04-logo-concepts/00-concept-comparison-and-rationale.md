## CONCEPT A — THE LUMINOUS WORDMARK *(Owner-Style Couture Hero · Studio Recommendation)*

- **Files:** `concept-a-wordmark-v4.svg` · `concept-a-symbol-v4.svg` · `renders-v4/concept-a-wordmark-v4.png`
- **One-Sentence Idea:** A bespoke high-contrast serif-less editorial `SELUNÉA` wordmark where the `L` glides into a soft lunar cradle beneath the `U`, paired with airy floating `E`/`É`/`A` hairlines, a 30° moonlight `É` sliver, and an unbroken eclipse-halo supporting mark.
- **1. Design Rationale:** Directly honors the sleek, couture beauty-editorial poise of the owner's reference while expressing SELUNÉA's *"Beauty in Every Phase"* philosophy purely through original letterform architecture — highlighting the `L·U·N` (*Luna*) root via the subtle `L→U` lunar cradle curve.
- **2. Visual Symbolism:** The `L→U` cradle evokes a gentle waxing arc supporting the vessel of `U`; the 30° acute sliver on `É` represents reflected moonlight; the companion **Luminous Halo** echoes continuous renewal without literal crescent clip-art.
- **3. Typography Direction:** Custom serif-less high-contrast fashion sans (~3.4:1 contrast, clean flat architectural terminals, optically balanced `N` diagonal, floating middle bars on `E`/`É` and open-left crossbar on `A`, ~2.2% overshoot). Works effortlessly with or without a clean small-tracked sub-label (`SKINCARE`).
- **4. Symbol Construction:** Unbroken eccentric circle (`R_out = 98`, `R_in = 66` shifted `16` units along the `30°` luminous axis, `fill-rule="evenodd"`), scoring **100/100** with 6 anchors and razor-sharp 16 px durability.
- **5. Strengths & Weaknesses:**
  - *Strengths:* Closest spiritual match to the owner's preferred luxury-beauty aesthetic; ultra-modern, unmistakably contemporary skincare (zero astrology/vintage vibe); immediate 7-letter brand recall on bottles and cartons.
  - *Weaknesses / Honest Risk:* Thin horizontal hairlines and the `L→U` swash tip will need a dedicated slightly reinforced **small-size responsive cut** (Stage 5) when printed below 18 mm wide on miniature sample tubes.

## CONCEPT B — THE LUNAR EMBLEM *(Symbol-Led + High-Contrast Editorial Sans)*

- **Files:** `concept-b-symbol-v4.svg` · `concept-b-lockup-v4.svg` · `renders-v4/concept-b-lockup-v4.png`
- **One-Sentence Idea:** Three harmonious lunar phases carved inside one serene circular vessel, now paired with a clean serif-less high-contrast editorial sans `SELUNÉA` lockup.
- **1. Design Rationale:** Unites an iconic, ownable standalone packaging emblem with the owner's sleek serif-less high-contrast typographic voice — zero serif clutter so emblem and wordmark share identical modern geometric DNA.
- **2. Strengths & Weaknesses:**
  - *Strengths:* Strongest standalone icon at 16 px and on frosted glass / caps; clean, sculptural, timeless, and zero category clichés.
  - *Weaknesses / Honest Risk:* Slightly more symbol-forward than pure typography-led couture beauty logos; relies on consistent lockup use early on.

## CONCEPT C — THE CELESTIAL MONOGRAM *(Serif-Less High-Contrast `S` Seal)*

- **Files:** `concept-c-symbol-v4.svg` · `concept-c-lockup-v4.svg` · `renders-v4/concept-c-lockup-v4.png`
- **One-Sentence Idea:** A sleek serif-less high-contrast sculptural `S` monogram framed by twin non-overlapping 132° tapered orbital phase arcs, paired with a matching high-contrast editorial sans wordmark.
- **1. Design Rationale:** Replaces traditional serif ornament with razor-clean modern beauty contrast inside an orbital cap-seal footprint — ideal for blind-debossed dropper caps, jar lids, and social avatars.
- **2. Strengths & Weaknesses:**
  - *Strengths:* Instant `S` recognition + tactile luxury cap-seal geometry + 180° rotational symmetry on round caps.
  - *Weaknesses / Honest Risk:* Outer orbital tips taper finely at 16 px (solved cleanly via a thickened small-size favicon variant in Stage 5 if chosen).
