## 1. Contemporary Skincare Competitive Landscape

To position **SELUNÉA** credibly in **Affordable Premium Skincare (Women 20–35)** without blending into neighbors or drifting into perfume/astrology, we map the category along two axes: **Clinical ↔ Poetic** and **Mass-Playful ↔ Niche-Luxury**.

### Four Visual Archetypes in Current Skincare
| Archetype | Representative Brands | Dominant Visual Cues | Strategic Gap for SELUNÉA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Clinical / Ingredient-First** | The Ordinary, Paula's Choice, CeraVe, Skintific | Cold white/grey grids, sterile Helvetica/grotesque sans, chemical formulas, clinical tables | Feels medicinal and emotionally dry; lacks daily self-care warmth and feminine poise |
| **2. Youthful / Gen-Z Pastel Pop** | Glossier, Glow Recipe, Rhode, Bubble | Thick rounded sans, candy pastels, glossy fruit/dew photography, playful stickers | Can feel overly juvenile or trend-dependent for 25–35 consumers seeking timeless refinement |
| **3. Quiet Apothecary / Botanical Luxury** | Aesop, L:A Bruket, Le Labo, Grown Alchemist | Amber glass, typewriter/monospace labels, dense text walls, austere sepia/black | High perceived price barrier; feels unisex-industrial rather than luminous and softly feminine |
| **4. Quiet Editorial / Modern Feminine Luxury** | Summer Fridays, Merit, Gisou, Rare Beauty, Refy, Nécessaire | Warm ivory/sand neutrals, wide-tracked custom serifs, sculptural monochrome marks, tactile frosted glass & matte tubes | **Closest peer neighborhood** — SELUNÉA belongs here, differentiated by *subtle lunar-phase geometry*, *cool-warm mineral harmony* (Ivory + Ash + Champagne/Silver), and an *affordable-premium* approachable voice |

### Category Clichés Identified (Strictly Off-Limits)
1. **The Astrology Crescent + Star Cliché:** Thin golden crescent cradling a 4-point sparkle (instantly reads as tarot, candles, or indie jewelry rather than dermatologist-trusted daily skincare).
2. **The Minimalist Monoline Leaf/Drop:** Generic single-line leaf or serum droplet (indistinguishable across thousands of private-label beauty brands).
3. **The Unmodified Didot/Playfair All-Caps Wordmark:** Default fashion serif with wide tracking and zero letterform customization (zero brand ownership or trademark defensibility).
4. **The Purple-Gold "Night Cream" Gradient:** Overused deep violet + gold sheen signaling dated anti-aging cosmetics rather than modern 20–35 daily ritual skincare.

## 2. Reference Library Research (`logo-design` 1,432-Logo Catalog)

We audited the bundled 1,432-logo library using `search_library.py` across SELUNÉA's key subjects, techniques, and typographic territories to benchmark craft standards and prevent accidental visual resemblance:

### A. Lunar / Crescent / Orbit Queries (`moon`, `crescent`, `orbit`)
- **Findings:** Only 5 matches for `moon`/`crescent` and 17 for `orbit`.
- **Key Structural Lessons:**
  - `dreamhost.svg` (★ exemplary): Proves a crescent built from **two pure circles** reads cleanly at small sizes, *but* literal circle-crescent silhouettes are visually close to existing marks — SELUNÉA must abstract lunar phases further (e.g., negative-space phase progression, nested light-shadow duality, or orbital symmetry) rather than drawing a simple two-circle crescent.
  - `lua.svg` & `jupyter.svg` (★ exemplary): Show orbital arcs with dots. Note how thin orbital arcs with small moons look distinctly *scientific/astronomy* and degrade below 24 px. Any orbital cue in SELUNÉA must use robust, sculptural weight and refined curvature so it stays unmistakably *beauty/editorial*.
  - `scaledrone.svg` & `moon.svg`: Confirm that purple/gradient crescents look dated and digital — reinforcing SELUNÉA's flat monochrome-first rule.

### B. Serif Typography Queries (`--type-style serif`)
- **Findings:** Only **17 logos (~3% of type-containing marks)** in the library use serif typography (49% use geometric sans).
- **Key Structural Lessons:**
  - `koa.svg`: High-contrast thin serif wordmark — visually refined at large size, but warns of hairline fragility at small scales.
  - `medium.svg` & `dinersclub.svg`: Show how pairing crisp **circular/elliptical geometry** with editorial serif proportion creates high memorability (`medium.svg`'s progression of circle → ellipse → narrow ellipse is a great structural study in *visual rhythm without literalism*).
  - **Implication:** In digital/e-commerce ecosystems dominated by geometric sans, a **custom editorial serif** immediately signals elevated beauty authority — provided stroke contrast is carefully engineered for 16–24 px digital avatars and small-tube silkscreen printing.

### C. Letterform `S` & Negative-Space Exemplary Queries
- **Findings:** 54 exemplary negative-space marks and 218 letterform marks audited (`kotlin-icon.svg`, `circleci.svg`, `gatsby.svg`, `svelte-icon.svg`, `zendesk-icon.svg`, `ello.svg`, `patreon.svg`).
- **Key Structural Lessons:**
  - **Extreme geometric economy wins:** The strongest marks (`circleci.svg`, `gatsby.svg`, `patreon.svg`, `svelte-icon.svg`) average **20–50 anchor points** (library square-icon median: 53; p25: 31).
  - **Letter `S` mechanics:** `svelte-icon.svg` shows an `S` built from interlocking curves with strong negative space, while `susy.svg` shows a classic serif `S`. Because `S` is a high-frequency brand initial, SELUNÉA's `S` monogram must carry an **ownable structural twist** (e.g., dual-phase negative space or continuous orbital balance) and still pass the 1-second Letter Test ("it unmistakably reads as `S`").

## 3. Name Mechanics & Word Mapping

### Linguistic & Structural Anatomy of `SELUNÉA`
- **Length:** 7 letters — ideal length for a standalone editorial wordmark (compact enough to sit boldly across a 30 ml serum bottle without shrinking into an illegible strip).
- **Phonetic Rhythm:** `Se – lu – né – a` — soft sibilant opening (`S`), liquid flow (`L-U-N`), luminous lifted accent (`É`), open feminine vowel resolution (`A`).
- **Rich Letterform Opportunities:**
  - **`S`** (opening anchor): Natural S-curve mirrors fluidity, skin contour, and waxing/waning wave rhythm — ideal monogram letter.
  - **`É`** (signature focal point): The acute accent sits at the golden-ratio area of the 7-letter word (5th of 7 letters), acting as a natural *luminous highlight* without adding extraneous icons.
  - **`U` & `N`**: Rhythmic inverted arch pair (`U` cup / `N` arch) creating calm visual cadence.
  - **`L` → `U` and `É` → `A`**: Key kerning pairs requiring optical refinement so the word reads as one serene breath.

### Word Map (Language → Abstract Visual Form)
```
SELUNÉA (Name)
 ├── Selene / Moon ──► phases, shadow-and-light terminator, eclipse edge, gibbous curve, quiet glow, tide
 ├── Flowing / Soft ──► ribbon, S-curve, silk, fluid meniscus, water ripple, skin contour
 └── É (Accent)    ──► slant of moonlight, slanted terminal, delicate spark/sliver, breath mark

SKINCARE RITUAL (Offering)
 ├── Daily Cycle   ──► morning/evening duality, repetition, steady rhythm, continuity, balance
 ├── Skin & Touch  ──► tactile matte, translucent frosted glass, pearl luster, softness, barrier
 └── Care          ──► cradling arc, gentle enclosure, vessel, calm stillness

ADJECTIVES (Refined · Feminine · Calm · Confident · Timeless)
 ├── Calm          ──► generous negative space, horizontal poise, low visual noise, quiet symmetry
 ├── Confident     ──► clear silhouette, deliberate thick–thin contrast, poised verticals, grounded ash ink
 └── Timeless      ──► pure circular geometry, classical typographic proportions, flat 1-color strength
```

### High-Value Intersections (Seeds for Stage 2–3 Exploration)
1. **Accent as Light (`É`):** Sculpting the acute accent as a crisp lunar light-sliver echoed subtly in the `S` terminal — typography carries the lunar story with zero clip-art.
2. **Terminator Line (Light & Shadow):** Expressing moon phases not as a stereotypical thin banana-crescent, but as a serene circular vessel divided by a soft negative-space curve (waxing/waning duality = morning/evening skin ritual).
3. **Orbital `S` Flow:** Two complementary phase arcs forming a sculptural `S` monogram whose negative space suggests gentle orbital balance — equally refined as a blind-debossed bottle cap seal or a 16 px favicon.

## 4. Mark-Type Strategic Assessment

Following `references/mark-types.md` §12, **SELUNÉA** has a short (7-letter), distinctive, newly introduced name that must live both on wide digital headers and on tiny cylindrical packaging caps/avatars:
- **Primary Brand System Hypothesis:** A **Wordmark + Companion Symbol/Monogram System** (Combination Architecture) — where the wordmark establishes name recognition on bottles/cartons/website, while the compact brand mark / `S` monogram anchors bottle caps, jar seals, tissue patterns, and 16 px–32 px digital avatars.
- Exploring **three genuinely different lead roles** in Stage 3 — (A) Typography-led Wordmark, (B) Symbol-led Abstract Lunar Emblem, and (C) Letterform-led Celestial `S` Monogram — ensures the client evaluates real strategic alternatives rather than superficial font swaps.
