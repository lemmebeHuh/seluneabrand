## 1. Visual & Material Art Direction ("Lunar Elegance")

To ensure SELUNÉA reads instantly as **affordable-premium contemporary skincare** (never astrology, perfume, or generic spa), every brand touchpoint is anchored in four tactile codes:

1. **Matte Mineral Surfaces (Moon Ivory `#F3F0E9`):** Soft-touch uncoated folding cartons and matte ceramic-feel cleanser/sunscreen tubes. Reflects light softly without plastic gloss.
2. **Frosted Translucent Vessels:** Acid-etched frosted glass bottles for Serum and Toner that diffuse product light like moonlight through mist — showing formulation honesty without harsh clinical transparency.
3. **Satin Metallic Precision (Champagne Beige `#C8B9A6` & Lunar Silver `#B8B7B1`):** Reserved exclusively for subtle collar rings, satin-brushed dropper accents, fine micro-foil stamping on cartons, and quiet hairlines — never heavy gold frames.
4. **Editorial Skin & Light Photography:** Soft 45° morning/dusk directional light, real skin pores and natural radiance (no airbrushed plastic skin), subtle glass caustics, cool-warm neutral stone/plaster props, and generous negative space (≥40% open breathing room).

## 2. Comparative Typography Study

All five candidate families from `selenua.md` are **SIL Open Font License (OFL)** — commercially safe for brand systems, web, and packaging (with the final logo wordmark custom-drawn as pure vector `<path>` outlines, never live `<text>`).

### A. Primary Editorial Serif Comparison (Display & Logo Reference)
| Typeface | Visual Character on `SELUNÉA` | Strengths | Weaknesses / Risks | Stage 2 Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cormorant Garamond** | Classical high-contrast humanist/garalde serif; lyrical curves on `S`, sculpted wedge terminals | Warm, effortlessly feminine, poetic editorial beauty tone; wide weight range (300–700) + true italics | Hairlines are delicate in Light/Regular weights; requires Medium/SemiBold (500–600) or custom hairline reinforcement for packaging print | **Leading Primary Display Face** (headlines, campaign copy, brand story) + structural warmth reference for custom wordmark |
| **Bodoni Moda** | Architectural modern Didone; crisp vertical stress, geometric ball terminals, optical size axis (`opsz`) | High editorial confidence, crisp symmetry, strong luxury fashion poise | Slightly cooler and more austere on its own; risks leaning toward fragrance/couture if not softened | **Secondary Structural Reference** for clean vertical precision in the custom logo wordmark |
| **Instrument Serif** | Condensed contemporary display serif; tall, narrow proportions | Editorial magazine feel, space-efficient on narrow serum bottles | Single weight only (Regular 400 + Italic); condensed rhythm feels trend-driven rather than timeless for a flagship wordmark | **Not recommended** as primary system face due to single-weight limitation |

### B. Secondary Functional Sans Comparison (UI, Body & Product Specs)
| Typeface | Visual Character | Strengths | Weaknesses | Stage 2 Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DM Sans** | Clean low-contrast geometric sans | Crisp, calm, understated; excels at uppercase tracked skincare category labels (`FACIAL CLEANSER · 120 ML`) and digital UI without competing with the serif | Slightly more neutral/technical in long body paragraphs | **Recommended for Packaging Specs & UI Labels** (or primary sans across whole system for cleanest restraint) |
| **Manrope** | Modern semi-geometric sans with subtle humanist warmth | Warm, approachable open counters; very comfortable for longer editorial paragraphs and e-commerce descriptions | Slightly softer/friendlier numerals than DM Sans on minimal packaging | **Strong Alternative** for body/digital copy; final single-sans selection (DM Sans vs. Manrope) will be locked in Stage 6 to maintain the strict **max 2 families** discipline |

## 3. Logo Concept Longlist (10 Distinct One-Sentence Ideas)

Per `logo-design` Phase 3, we explored **10 genuinely distinct structural concepts** across the three required territories before shortlisting the 3 strongest for Stage 3 SVG construction.

### Territory A — Typography-Led Wordmarks
1. **A1 · Luminous Diacritic Wordmark:** Custom balanced-contrast serif `SELUNÉA` where the `S` carries a subtle lunar-phase spine swell and the acute accent on `É` is sculpted into a poised moonlight sliver (paired with a minimal single-phase companion mark).
2. **A2 · Horizon Ligature Wordmark:** Custom wide-tracked serif `SELUNÉA` where the `L` base extends gently beneath `U` like a calm horizon line supporting a lunar basin.
3. **A3 · Stencil-Break Serif Wordmark:** High-fashion editorial `SELUNÉA` where diagonal light-breaks open the `S`, `E`, and `A` counters like moonlight slicing through shadow.

### Territory B — Symbol-Led Abstract Lunar Emblems
4. **B1 · Phase Equilibrium Emblem (Negative-Space Vessel):** A poised geometric circle divided by a calm S-curved terminator channel (real `evenodd` negative space) into two complementary waxing/waning hemispheres — expressing cyclical skin balance without a cliché crescent.
5. **B2 · Tri-Phase Progression Emblem:** Three graduated geometric shapes (thin sliver → half-disc → full circle) aligned on a serene horizontal axis — pure abstract rhythm of moon phases and multi-step skincare ritual.
6. **B3 · Eclipse Ring & Halo Emblem:** Two subtly offset concentric rings creating a continuous varying-width luminous halo (full circle outside, gentle crescent void inside) — minimal, architectural, and jewel-like without looking like jewelry.
7. **B4 · Polar Orbit Arc Emblem:** Two mirrored open quarter-arcs embracing a quiet central void — expressing calm protection and cyclical renewal.

### Territory C — Letterform / Celestial `S` Monograms
8. **C1 · Sculptural Orbital `S` Monogram:** A custom high-contrast `S` whose upper and lower bowls are constructed from opposing lunar phase arcs, subtly balanced by a single calibrated orbital curve/point — engineered specifically for embossed bottle caps, jar seals, and 16 px favicons.
9. **C2 · Continuous Ribbon `S` Monogram:** A single unbroken monoline `S` looping into a vertical infinity-orbit — symbolizing continuous day-and-night self-care.
10. **C3 · Framed Seal `S` Crest:** A classical serif `S` locked inside an elliptical lunar-phase double ring — heritage apothecary seal feel.

## 5. Architectural Blueprints Ready for Stage 3 (Black-First SVG Construction)

All three selected concepts will be built in **pure solid black (`#111111` / `#000000`) on white**, using clean integer `viewBox` coordinates (`256×256` symbols; height `256` lockups), **pure `<path>` outlines** (zero `<text>`, zero `<filter>`, zero rasters, real `fill-rule="evenodd"` cutouts), and audited via `svg_audit.py` + `preview_sheet.py` + `concept_sheet.py`:

1. **CONCEPT A — THE LUMINOUS WORDMARK (`A1`)**
   - **Lead Role:** Typography-led editorial identity.
   - **Construction:** Custom-drawn serif `SELUNÉA` (calibrated ~3.5:1 stem-to-hairline ratio so thin strokes survive frosted-glass printing), distinctive calligraphic-geometric `S` with lunar-curve inflection, acute accent on `É` carved as an upward-angled light sliver (30° axis), optically kerned `L-U` and `É-A`, accompanied by a minimal 2-element supporting phase mark (open luminous arc + light-sliver dot) for 16 px / cap use.
2. **CONCEPT B — THE LUNAR EMBLEM (`B1`)**
   - **Lead Role:** Symbol-led abstract lunar geometry.
   - **Construction:** Pure circular outer silhouette (`r = 100` on `256×256` canvas) unified with a sculptural negative-space S-curve phase channel + nested waning counter-arc (`fill-rule="evenodd"`), evoking waxing/waning equilibrium and skin renewal in a single calm geometric gesture — zero literal moon clip-art, zero stars, ≥14-unit minimum gap so it stays crisp at 16 px.
3. **CONCEPT C — THE CELESTIAL MONOGRAM (`C1`)**
   - **Lead Role:** Custom `S` letterform monogram engineered for bottle caps, seals & favicons.
   - **Construction:** Organic-yet-structured `S` built from two balanced circular phase bowls joined by a fluid spine and framed by a subtle, open 240° orbital arc (or crescent-counter equilibrium) that guides the eye without clutter — immediately passes the 1-second Letter Test (`S`) at 16 px while feeling bespoke and tactile when blind-debossed on a 14 mm serum cap.
