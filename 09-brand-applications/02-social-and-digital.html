Art Direction & Editorial Photography SystemCampaign Visual Principles
01 · Soft Directional Lighting

Low-angle 45° morning or twilight light casting long, diffused shadows across travertine, matte plaster, and frosted glass vessels.

02 · Authentic Natural Skin

Real skin pores, calm luminosity, minimal makeup, diverse women aged 20–35. Strictly avoid plastic over-retouching or artificial sheen.

03 · Fluid & Pearl Surfaces

Subtle water ripples, frosted glass caustics, and warm neutral silk/ceramic reflections echoing quiet moonlight without glitter.

04 · Editorial Negative Space

Reserve ≥40% open Moon Ivory or warm stone breathing room in every composition so typography and vessels feel unhurried and serene.
