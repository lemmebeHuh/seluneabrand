The brief

Complete brand identity for SELUNÉA, an affordable premium contemporary skincare brand for women aged 20–35. Creative direction: Lunar Elegance — refined feminine minimalism expressed through custom high-contrast couture typography, subtle lunar-phase negative space, and tactile matte ivory / frosted glass packaging.

Final design

SELUNÉA — Lunar Elegance Identity System

A couture serif-less high-contrast SELUNÉA wordmark (with L→U lunar cradle swash & 30° É moonlight sliver) unified with a 100/100 Celestial Lotus-Moon Brand Mark and Celestial S Monogram.

  • Honors the owner's preferred sleek high-contrast fashion-editorial sans style while staying 100% original to SELUNÉA and its Moon Ivory / Midnight Ash / Champagne Beige palette.
  • Celestial Lotus-Moon Brand Mark scores 100/100 on automated vector audit with pure expanded outlines and survives 16 px favicons, frosted glass silkscreen, and Champagne foil jar lids effortlessly.
  • Includes dedicated irradiation-thinned reversed white artwork and reinforced small-size responsive cuts for 30 mL serum bottles and 16 px web icons.
