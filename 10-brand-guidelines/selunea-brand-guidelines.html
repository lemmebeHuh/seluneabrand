01–04 · Brand Introduction, Philosophy, Positioning & Personality

SELUNÉA is an affordable-premium contemporary skincare brand crafted for women aged 20–35. Rather than promising instant overnight miracles, Selunéa celebrates the evolving rhythm of real skin and the quiet power of consistent daily self-care.

01 · Brand Introduction & 02 · Philosophy

Inspired by the phases of the moon, Selunéa reflects how skin naturally shifts across stress, climate, and monthly cycles. Every formulation and visual touchpoint expresses calm equilibrium — anchored by our core message “Beauty in Every Phase.” and supporting tagline “Skincare for every version of you.”

03 · Brand Positioning

Affordable Premium Skincare: Occupies the refined editorial territory between cold clinical ingredient brands and inaccessible heritage luxury. Visually composed and tactile (frosted glass, matte ivory, disciplined negative space), yet emotionally warm and inviting for daily use.

04 · Brand Personality

  • Refined & Sophisticated
  • Feminine & Approachable
  • Calm, Confident & Timeless

Creative Pillars (Embrace)

  • Modern feminine minimalism
  • Subtle lunar geometry & negative space
  • High-contrast couture editorial clarity

Category Guardrails (Avoid)

  • Generic crescent moon & star clip-art
  • Astrology, tarot, or purple galaxy gradients
  • Generic leaves or heavy gold framing

13 · Editorial Photography Art Direction

Lighting:

Soft 45° directional morning/dusk glow; diffused glass caustics; zero harsh flash.

Skin Authenticity:

Natural pores, healthy hydration, real women 20–35; never over-retouched plastic skin.

Materials & Props:

Matte limestone, travertine, warm linen, frosted glass, subtle fluid water ripples.

Composition:

≥40% calm negative space; warm Moon Ivory & neutral stone tones; editorial stillness.

15 · Social Media & Digital Governance

  • Profile Avatar: White Lunar Brand Mark centered on Midnight Ash circular tile (#34332F).
  • Feed Rhythm: Alternate macro frosted-glass product stills, natural skin editorial portraits, and quiet Moon Ivory typographic quote cards (never overcrowded text).
  • Stories & Campaigns: Pair Cormorant Garamond italic headlines with DM Sans tracked phase tags; keep ≥35% clean margin around edges.
  • Legal & Pre-Press Notice: Conduct formal trademark clearance & physical Pantone/foil press proofs before mass packaging production.
